Who We Are

Focused, disciplined coaching built around one exam

Expert Navodaya Coaching Centre in MK Hubli was founded with a single purpose: help talented rural and semi-urban students earn a seat in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya through free, quality residential education. We understand the JNVST syllabus, pattern, and pace better than most, because it is all we teach.

Our teaching blends both English and Kannada, so no student is held back by language — only by how much they practise. Small batches, weekly mock tests, and a hostel for outstation students make this a complete preparation environment, not just a tuition class.

Hostel Facility

A safe home away from home for outstation students

Many bright students come from villages far from MK Hubli. Our supervised hostel lets them focus fully on preparation without the daily travel burden.

  • Separate, secure accommodation with warden supervision
  • Two meals a day, plus morning breakfast and evening snacks
  • Filtered drinking water
  • Dedicated evening study hours with staff support
  • Clean rooms and basic amenities
  • Regular communication with parents on progress
